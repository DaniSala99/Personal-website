# About — version A (Sito Framer + LinkedIn)

In September 2025, the Seveso and Bevera rivers overflowed across Northern
Lombardy. I was on the operational team running the hydrological alerts,
post-event impact assessment and institutional reporting that fed directly
into Regione Lombardia's emergency declarations. That kind of work — where
hydrological models meet real consequences for real cities — is exactly
what I do as a freelancer too: flood risk modeling, climate data analytics
and CSRD-aligned physical risk for engineering firms, insurers and
climate-tech teams across Europe.
