**Master Thesis:** Impact of Agricultural Irrigation on the Hydrologic Regime of the Po River Basin  
Supervisor: Prof. Giovanni Ravazzani, DICA

*Research objectives and methodology:*
- Performed catchment-scale water balance and water accounting analysis, quantifying irrigation impacts on the hydrological cycle
- Developed and calibrated the FEST hydrological model to simulate land surface and subsurface water dynamics across the Po River Basin, integrating 10 years of meteorological, hydrological, and land-use spatial data within a GIS framework
- Conducted comprehensive sensitivity and uncertainty analysis on key model parameters and developed counterfactual scenarios contrasting current irrigation practices, alternative withdrawal strategies and drought conditions, isolating the contribution of human water-use decisions from purely climatic drivers

*Key findings:*
- Quantified 15% reduction in river flows during irrigation season (April–September), with altered evapotranspiration patterns and decreased aquifer levels throughout the year
- Simulated and evaluated drought-stress scenarios by systematically varying irrigation withdrawal intensity, providing quantitative insight into soil moisture depletion dynamics and groundwater recharge deficits under water-limited conditions

*Technical skills applied:* Process-based numerical modelling (FEST, Fortran) · GIS-based spatial analysis · thematic and hazard mapping · environmental time-series processing · scenario development under climate stress conditions
